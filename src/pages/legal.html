Risk disclosure

Capital is at risk. Orrith products finance real shipping cash flows. Returns depend on the performance of a voyage and the payment of an invoice by a third party. You may lose some or all of your capital.

  • Credit risk. The buyer of the cargo, or another obligor, may fail to pay. Guarantees and insurance reduce this risk but do not remove it; a guarantor or insurer may dispute or delay a claim.
  • Operational risk. A voyage may be delayed, diverted or cancelled. Cargo may be lost or damaged. The operator may fail to perform.
  • Liquidity risk. Products are fixed-term. There is no secondary market and no early redemption.
  • Counterparty and structural risk. Investors are exposed to the issuer, the operator, insurers, guarantors, custodians and the tokenisation infrastructure used to distribute the product.
  • Technology risk. Onchain infrastructure may contain defects or be subject to attack. Stablecoins may lose their peg.
  • Legal and regulatory risk. The treatment of tokenised investment products varies by jurisdiction and may change.

Returns stated on this site are fixed contractual returns for a specific product and cycle, not projections of future performance. Past performance, where any exists, is not a guide to future results.
