The gap

The biggest market in the world still raises money one voyage at a time.

Ship finance goes to large owners through a handful of European banks. Trade finance goes to large corporates. The operator in the middle, the one actually running the voyage, borrows from private lenders at a cost that eats the margin.

Meanwhile, billions in stablecoins sit onchain looking for yield backed by something real.

What Orrith does

Originate on the shipping side. Issue and distribute on the digital asset side.

01 — ORIGINATE

Voyages we can stand behind

We source voyages and receivables from operators we know, and underwrite them with people who have run ships.

02 — STRUCTURE

One vault per cash flow

Each product is a fixed-term vault with a named obligor, defined collateral and insurance in place before capital moves.

03 — DISTRIBUTE

Stablecoins in, stablecoins out

Investors deposit stablecoins into the vault and are repaid, with return, when the invoice settles.

How it works

Four steps. Forty-five days.

STEP 1

Capital pooled

Investors commit stablecoins to the Orrith vault for one fixed cycle.

STEP 2

Voyage financed

Orrith advances the funds to the operator against the insured voyage invoice.

STEP 3

Cargo delivered

The cargo reaches port and the buyer pays the invoice on delivery.

STEP 4

Capital returned

The lock ends and investors are repaid capital plus the fixed return.

  • Settlement in minutes, not banking days
