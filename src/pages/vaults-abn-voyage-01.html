04 — Collateral and insurance

What stands behind the capital

  • The invoice. Orrith advances funds against the voyage invoice and is repaid from it on delivery.
  • Cargo and voyage insurance. Cover is in place before any capital is deployed.
  • Bank guarantee on the buyer. If the buyer fails to pay once the cargo reaches port, the guarantee is called.
  • Operator skin in the game. ABN Shipping funds 50% of the remaining voyage cost itself, so it shares any loss.

08 — FAQ

Questions investors ask first.

What exactly am I financing?

One invoice for one containerised oil shipment from Dubai to Indonesia, carried by ABN Shipping Services. Your capital is advanced against that invoice and repaid from it when the cargo is delivered and the buyer pays.

Where does the 4.5% come from?

From the margin embedded in the voyage. The operator pays Orrith a financing charge for funding the trip up front instead of raising from several private lenders at a higher cost. That charge, after Orrith's fee, is the investor return.

When do I get my money back?

On day 46, once the 45-day lock ends. Capital and the 4.5% return are paid together in USDT.

What if the buyer doesn't pay?

Payment is secured by a bank guarantee. If the buyer fails to pay once the cargo has reached port, the guarantee is called.

What if the cargo is lost?

The cargo and the voyage are insured before any capital is deployed. A loss in transit is an insurance claim, not an investor loss, up to the insured value.

Who is the issuer?

Catalysis International, Panama. Investors subscribe through the issuer after KYC. Orrith originates, structures and reports.

Who can invest?

Investors who pass KYC and eligibility checks. The vault is not available to US persons or in restricted jurisdictions.
