About

A shipping-native RWA company.

Orrith turns real shipping cash flows, voyage invoices, freight receivables, fuel, cargo and vessels, into onchain investment products, funded through stablecoin rails and vault infrastructure.

What we are

An asset manager and originator with onchain distribution. We sit between two worlds: shipping, the physical layer that carries most of world trade, and digital asset capital markets, where abundant capital is looking for real yield.

What we are not

Not a DeFi protocol. Not a token. Not a shipping line. We do not write our own vault contracts and we do not publish returns we cannot back.
