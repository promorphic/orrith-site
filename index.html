FAQ

Common Questions

Do my recipients need a crypto wallet?

No. Recipients receive local currency directly in their bank account or mobile wallet. Stablecoins are only used as the settlement layer between countries.

Which stablecoins does Orrith use?

We primarily settle in USDC and EURC, both fully reserve-backed and issued by regulated entities. Enterprise customers can request additional assets.

How is the exchange rate calculated?

We quote the mid-market rate plus a fixed, disclosed spread. The rate you see is the rate you get — locked for the duration of your quote.

Is my money safe while in transit?

Customer funds are held in segregated accounts and backed 1:1. Orrith never lends customer balances, and every transfer is tracked end-to-end.

How long does onboarding take?

Most businesses are approved within 2 business days. Sandbox API access is instant.
