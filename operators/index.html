What we need from you

Four things to start.

  • The invoice and the buyer. Who pays, how much, on what terms.
  • Insurance certificate. Cargo and voyage cover, or the quote you intend to bind.
  • Voyage plan. Route, vessel, cargo, laycan and expected delivery.
  • Last 12 months of voyages. What you carried, for whom, and how each invoice was paid.
