FAQ

Before the call.

Is this a token or a fund?

A fixed-term investment product issued by Catalysis International, Panama, and distributed through a KYC-gated onchain vault. Investor positions are represented by vault tokens. It is not a freely tradeable token and Orrith has no token of its own.

Why not build your own contracts?

Security. Orrith's edge is originating and underwriting shipping cash flows, not writing smart contracts. Products are deployed on established, audited tokenisation rails.

What is the track record?

Voyage 01 is the first Orrith vault. Our operating partner has 20 years at sea on the Gulf route. We do not publish projected or backtested returns; each vault's return is fixed at launch and stated on its page.

How is the return taxed or reported?

Investors receive subscription and repayment documentation from the issuer. Tax treatment depends on your jurisdiction; we do not give tax advice.
